However, if a ship has been taken into a shipyard in Sweden, the party responsible for operating the shipyard is responsible for such coordination. The responsibility for coordination of safety measures required for the loading or unloading of a ship in a Swedish port rests with the employer responsible for this work.</p> <p>The responsibility for coordination set forth in the first paragraph can be transferred to a party conducting activities at the workplace or, where the loading or unloading of a ship in a Swedish port is concerned, at the port, or the shipowner.</p> <p>With regard to shared workplaces other than those referred to in the first paragraph, those who are conducting activities there may agree that one of them will be responsible for such coordination.
